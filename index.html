Abstract

Research Overview

Parkinson's Disease (PD) is a progressive neurodegenerative disorder affecting over 10 million people worldwide. Early and accurate diagnosis remains a clinical challenge due to the subtlety of earlystage symptoms. This project presents a dualpathway machine learning framework that leverages both structured clinical data and acoustic vocal biomarkers to deliver reliable, non-invasive predictions. Six classification algorithms including Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, SVM, KNN, and Gradient Boosting were trained and evaluated under class-weighted balanced conditions. The final selected model, Gradient Boosting Classifier, delivered the highest generalisation performance and was deployed as a lightweight Django web application for rapid clinical screening.

Neurological Changes

Parkinson's Disease causes distinct changes in vocal cord motor control, producing measurable acoustic signatures detectable years before clinical motor symptoms fully emerge. Our voice model analyses MDVP biomarkers extracted from short voice recordings to classify Parkinson's risk with 89.74% accuracy on 39 test samples.
